Rooted in Lyari, Karachi

Passion for Flavor, Made for the Local Foodie.

Situated at Moosa Lane in the heart of historic Lyari, Cafe Bites was established with a singular vision: to serve high-quality, melt-in-your-mouth gourmet fast food without compromising on generosity or flavor.

From our signature cheese lava burgers and succulent beef patties to our hot crispy broast and aromatic Chinese chow mein, every single bite is prepared with fresh meat, hand-picked spices, and our special secret sauces.
